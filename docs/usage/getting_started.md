# Getting started

Mitup doesn't work by adding it to your group. While other bots might need to be added to a group to gain context and permissions (this might be needed if the bot is intende to monitor conversations, like moderate the group), Mitup does not need anything from the group and, therefore, it has been developed the goal of functioning entire in a private conversation with each user.

To start using the Mitup, just head to [@mitupbot] and start a conversation with it.

## Registration process

### Configuring the timezone

Once you start your conversation with Mitup, you will be greated by the following message:

!!! quote "Setting up the timezone"

    Welcome to Mitup Bot Alice!

    Let's start by setting your timezone. Send me the name of your city or, for a more accurate result, your location by pressing on 📎 and selecting Location.

    **Important**: we do not store your location and this information is only used to configure your timezone.

The first step in the registration process is to define your timezone. This is important because meetings created in Mitup can have a date and a time and users that have joined the meeting will be notified before the meeting starts. Mitup works all around the world and, in order to be sure that everyone involved in the meeting knows exactly when the meeting starts, each user needs to provide the timezone where they are located.

The timezone can be set in tow ways:

1. By providing the name of your city
2. By sending a message with your location :round_pushpin:

!!! note "Privacy Notice"

    Mitup does not store the location sent while configuring your timezone. This is used only to find the timezone of the location sent. Once the timezone is set, the location is discarded.

    For more information about what Mitup does with your data visit the [Privacy page](../faq/privacy.md)

Once your timezone has been set you will be greated by the main menu and you are noready to use Mitup!
