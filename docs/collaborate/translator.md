# Translate Mitup

Help bring Mitup to people in your language. Language shouldn't be a barrier to organizing with friends.

Read something in the bot that doesn't sound right in your language? Missing your language altogether? Both are worth telling us about, and both get fixed by people like you.

## How to get started

1. Tell us which language you want to help with, by email at [support@mitup.social](mailto:support@mitup.social) or in the Translations topic of the [community group on Telegram](https://t.me/mitupgroup). A string that reads wrong is welcome in the same message.
2. Visit the [Crowdin project](https://crowdin.com/project/mitup) and create a free account or log in.
3. Select your language.
4. Start translating strings that need help. Approved translations reach the bot with its next release.

For a language Mitup doesn't have yet, that first message is the whole first step: once someone fluent is on board, the language gets opened on Crowdin.

No translation experience required. Fluency in your target language and basic English is enough.

## Guidelines

When translating:

* Keep the friendly tone of the original.
* Preserve the meaning.
* Consider the context provided for each string.
* Use consistent terminology throughout.

## Why it matters

Every new language lets another group of friends use Mitup in the words they already speak. Your work is what grows the community beyond the languages it covers today.

Have questions about a string or a term? The Translations topic of the [community group on Telegram](https://t.me/mitupgroup) is open to anyone using Mitup.
