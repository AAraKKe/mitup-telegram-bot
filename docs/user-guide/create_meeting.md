# Create your first meeting :sparkles:

Once you are registered with Mitup, creating a new meeting is simple!

Here's the step-by-step process:

1.  **Start the creation**: Tap the *:heavy_plus_sign: New meeting*{.button-like} button, usually found in the bot's main menu.
2.  **Name your meeting**: The bot will ask you for a title. Just send a message with the name you want for your meeting (e.g., "Weekend Hike Prep", "Board Game Night", "Ana's Birthday Drinks").
3.  **Meeting Created!**: Mitup instantly creates the meeting using the title you provided. It also applies your [default meeting settings](configuration.md#default-meeting-options) (you can change these defaults in your user settings!).
4.  **Edit and Refine**: Right after creation, the bot will show you a message confirming the meeting is created, along with buttons to edit its details. See the [Configure Your Meeting](configure_meeting.md) guide for all the options available when editing.

That's it! Your meeting is created and ready to be configured further or shared.
