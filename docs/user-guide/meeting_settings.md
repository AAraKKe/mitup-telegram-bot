# Meeting settings

Open a meeting and tap *⚙️ Settings*{.button-like} under the card. Three things live there: the language the meeting speaks, how it behaves, and how its dates and times are written. The title, the schedule, the place, the photos and the guest list are edited [on the card itself](create_a_meeting.md#the-meeting-card).

## Language

A meeting's language is its own, independent of yours: you can run the bot in English and share a meeting that greets your friends in Spanish. The menus you navigate stay in [your language](settings.md#language).

### Waiting list

People who join a full meeting queue up and move in as spots free up, in the order they joined. With it off, a join on a full meeting is turned away.

### Public

Lets anyone who receives the card share it on. With it off, the card stays in the chats you post it in yourself.

### Open invitations

Puts *😄 Invite*{.button-like} on the card, so participants can sign up friends, even friends who are not on Telegram.

### Incognito

Keeps the names off the shared card; only the count shows. You still see everyone on your own card.

### Lock on start

Freezes the guest list once the meeting starts, so nobody joins or leaves while it runs. With an end time the freeze lifts at the end; without one it holds until you turn the lock off or the meeting goes inactive. It needs a start time.

## Time format

Three controls: whether the timezone is written next to the times, a 24-hour or a 12-hour clock, and the date format, with a preview of each choice.

Times are written in your timezone, the one from [your settings](settings.md#timezone), and what you pick here is what everyone who sees the card reads. On Telegram clients that support it, a date or a time on the card is tappable and shows the moment on the reader's own clock.

## Default options

[Your own settings](settings.md#default-meeting-options) hold the defaults every new meeting starts with: the same five switches and the same time format.
