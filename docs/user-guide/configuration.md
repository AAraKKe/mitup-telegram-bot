## Language

Tap *🔣 Language*{.button-like} to choose your preferred language. All bot messages, buttons, and notifications will appear in that language.

## Timezone

Tap *🌐 Timezone*{.button-like} to set your timezone. You can send a city name or share your location via Telegram. This ensures Mitup displays all meeting times accurately, which is especially important when joining meetings across timezones.

## Reminders

Tap *⏰ Notifications*{.button-like} to configure reminders. Toggle them on or off, and set how many minutes before a meeting you want to be notified. The default is 5 minutes.

## Timeout

Tap *⌛ Timeout*{.button-like} to set how long meetings stay active in your list after they end. When this period expires, meetings move to your past meetings. You can reactivate them if needed.

## Default meeting options

These pre-selected settings apply to new meetings you create (you can change them per meeting).

Tap *👥 Default Options*{.button-like} to toggle:

### Waiting list

Allow people to join even when the meeting is full. They go on a waiting list and move to active when spots open.

### Public

Let anyone who gets your invite share it with others.

### Open invitations

Let participants add friends to the meeting, even if those friends aren't on Telegram.

### Incognito

Hide the participant list when your meeting is shared (show only the total count). As the creator, you always see the full list.

### Show timezone

Control whether timezone info appears in meeting messages. When off, both the timezone info and timezone button are hidden.

## Privacy

Tap *🛡️ Privacy*{.button-like} to adjust settings that control your personal information within Mitup.
