# Limits and Host perks

Mitup is free to use, and a few limits keep it that way: every meeting and every participant is a row in a database someone has to pay for. The caps are wide enough that a normal plan with friends never meets them. If you run something bigger, backing Mitup on Patreon raises or removes them.

The people who back Mitup are called Hosts, and each [tier](../collaborate/donation.md) maps to a set of limits:

| | Free and Brewer | Gamemaster | Commissioner |
|---|---|---|---|
| Active meetings you own | 5 | 50 | Unlimited |
| How far ahead you can schedule | 90 days | 365 days | No limit |
| Participants per meeting | 20 | No cap | No cap |

The Brewer tier is a pure thank-you: it comes with the badge and the members-only group, and keeps the same limits as a free account.

## Active meetings

You can own up to 5 active meetings at once. Meetings you joined don't count, only the ones you created, and neither do inactive ones sitting in *💾 Your past meetings*{.button-like}. The cap is checked when you create a meeting and when you reactivate an inactive one:

!!! quote "At the cap"

    You've reached your limit of 5 active meetings. Wrap up or delete one to start another, or tap Collaborate in the main menu to support Mitup and unlock more.

A meeting leaves the count as soon as it becomes inactive, so letting a finished meeting close out (see [meeting lifecycle](meeting_lifecycle.md)) frees the slot on its own.

## Scheduling ahead

A meeting's start date can be up to 90 days out. Pick a date past that and the bot asks for an earlier one:

!!! quote "Too far out"

    You can schedule meetings up to 90 days ahead for now. Pick an earlier date, or tap Collaborate in the main menu to support Mitup and schedule further out.

Gamemasters can schedule up to a year ahead, and Commissioners as far as they like.

## Participants per meeting

A free meeting holds up to 20 participants, even when you leave the participant limit open. Setting an explicit limit above 20 isn't possible either:

!!! quote "Over the capacity"

    Free meetings can host up to 20 participants. Set a lower limit, or tap Collaborate in the main menu to support Mitup and host more.

Once a meeting is full, further joins go to the [waiting list](meeting_settings.md#waiting-list) when it's on, or are turned away when it's off. Gamemaster and Commissioner meetings have no participant cap at all.

!!! note "Nobody gets kicked out"

    If a meeting is already over a cap, everyone on the list stays. That can happen when a Host tier lapses. The meeting simply reads as full until it drops back under the cap.

## Raising the limits

Back Mitup on Patreon and link your account through *♥ Collaborate*{.button-like} in the main menu. The higher limits switch on automatically, no matter whether you pledge first or link first. The [supporting Mitup](../collaborate/donation.md) page covers the tiers and how linking works.
