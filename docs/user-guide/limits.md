# Limits and Host perks

Mitup is free to use, and a few limits keep it that way: every meeting and every participant is a row in a database someone has to pay for. The caps are wide enough that a normal plan with friends never meets them. If you run something bigger, backing Mitup on Patreon raises or removes them.

The people who back Mitup are called Hosts, and each [tier](../collaborate/donation.md) maps to a set of limits:

| | Free | Brewer | Gamemaster | Commissioner |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Active meetings you own | 5 | 5 | 50 | Unlimited |
| How far ahead you can schedule | 90 days | 90 days | 365 days | No limit |
| Participants per meeting | 20 | 20 | No cap | No cap |
| A meeting with no date stays active | 90 days | 90 days | 365 days | 365 days |
| A finished meeting is kept | 90 days | 90 days | 365 days | 365 days |
| Photos on the meeting card | <span class="badge no">no</span> | <span class="badge yes">up to 5</span> | <span class="badge yes">up to 5</span> | <span class="badge yes">up to 5</span> |

The Brewer tier is mostly a thank-you: it keeps the same caps as a free account, and adds the badge, the Hosts-only group, and photos on your meeting cards.

## Active meetings

You can own up to 5 active meetings at once. Meetings you joined don't count, only the ones you created, and neither do inactive ones sitting in *💾 Past*{.button-like}. The cap is checked when you create a meeting and when you reactivate an inactive one; at the cap, the bot turns the new one down and says why. A meeting leaves the count as soon as it becomes inactive, so letting a finished meeting close out (see [meeting lifecycle](meeting_lifecycle.md)) frees the slot on its own.

## Scheduling ahead

A meeting's start date can be up to 90 days out. Pick a date past that and the bot asks for an earlier one. Gamemasters can schedule up to a year ahead, and Commissioners as far as they like.

## Participants per meeting

A free meeting holds up to 20 participants, even when you leave the participant limit open. Setting a limit above 20 isn't possible either. Once a meeting is full, further joins go to the [waiting list](meeting_settings.md#waiting-list) when it's on, or are turned away when it's off. Gamemaster and Commissioner meetings have no participant cap at all.

!!! note "Nobody gets kicked out"

    If a meeting is already over a cap, everyone on the list stays. That can happen when a Host tier lapses. The meeting reads as full until it drops back under the cap.

## Meetings with no date

A meeting with no date stays active so you can add one later, and nothing else will ever finish it, so it gets a clock of its own: 90 days on a free or Brewer account, a year for Gamemasters and Commissioners. The count runs from when you created the meeting, or from the last time you brought it back from *💾 Past*{.button-like}. When it runs out the meeting becomes inactive and moves into that list, where you can reactivate it.

## How long a finished meeting is kept

An inactive meeting sits in *💾 Past*{.button-like} for 90 days, or a year on the Gamemaster and Commissioner tiers, and is then deleted permanently. A week before that the bot sends you one heads-up with a button to bring the meeting back. The [meeting lifecycle](meeting_lifecycle.md#how-long-an-inactive-meeting-is-kept) page follows the whole path.

!!! note "The tier you're on right now decides"

    Both of those windows are measured against your current tier, not the one you were on when
    the meeting was created. Become a Gamemaster and the finished meetings you already have
    stretch to a year. If your support lapses, they go back to 90 days.

## How long one meeting can last

A meeting can span a week at most, from its start to its end. That one is the same on every account, Commissioners included. Set an end further out and the bot asks for a closer one. For an all-weekend trip or a three-day tournament, one meeting covers it. For something that runs longer, make a meeting per session.

## The timeout grace period

Your timeout, the time a meeting stays active after it finishes, can be up to a day (1440 minutes) on every tier. See [Your settings](settings.md#timeout).

## Raising the limits

Back Mitup on Patreon and link your account through *♥ Collaborate*{.button-like} in the main menu. The higher limits switch on automatically, no matter whether you pledge first or link first. The [supporting Mitup](../collaborate/donation.md) page covers the tiers and how linking works.

## Photos on the meeting card

Hosts can put photos on a meeting, up to 5 of them, on every tier from Brewer up. They sit at the top of the card, so the people you share it with see the trailhead or the board game before they read a word. [Your meeting card](create_a_meeting.md#images) covers adding, replacing and arranging them.

## Hosts-only group

Every Host, from Brewer up, gets a seat in a private Telegram group, joined from *♥ Collaborate*{.button-like}. The [supporting Mitup](../collaborate/donation.md#joining-the-hosts-only-group) page has the walkthrough.
