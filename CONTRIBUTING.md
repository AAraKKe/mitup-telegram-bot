# Contributing to Mitup

Contributions are welcome in many forms: code, translations, docs, and bug reports all help.

One rule holds before you write code: contributions start from an issue. Find one that is ready, or open a new one at the [template chooser](https://gitlab.com/meetupbot/mitup-telegram-bot/-/issues/new/choose), and wait for a maintainer to mark it ready.

The full handbook is published at [mitup.social](https://mitup.social):

* **[Handbook overview](https://mitup.social/collaborate/code_contributor/):** where to start and how the guides fit together.
* **[Setup guide](https://mitup.social/contribute/setup/):** set up your local environment and run the bot.
* **[Contribution workflow](https://mitup.social/contribute/making_contributions/):** from an accepted issue to a merged merge request.
* **[Other ways to help](https://mitup.social/collaborate/donation/):** support the project financially, or [translate Mitup](https://mitup.social/collaborate/translator/) into your language.
* **[Code of conduct](https://mitup.social/collaborate/code_of_conduct/):** the standards everyone taking part agrees to.

AI-assisted contributions are welcome, and [you own every line you submit](https://mitup.social/contribute/working_with_agents/), however it was produced.

By contributing, you agree that your contributions will be licensed under the [MIT License](https://gitlab.com/meetupbot/mitup-telegram-bot/-/blob/main/LICENSE).
